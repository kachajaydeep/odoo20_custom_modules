Point of Sale Weight and Volume

Point of Sale Weight and Volume enhances the standard Odoo Point of Sale by allowing you to maintain Weight and Volume information for products and display these values throughout the POS workflow.
This module is especially useful for supermarkets, grocery stores, hardware shops, agriculture businesses, manufacturing outlets, wholesale stores, chemical suppliers, and any business where product weight or volume information is important during sales.

📦 POS Order Line Weight & Volume Workflow

Point Of Sale → Click "Open Register"

Display weight and volume for each POS order line and see total weight and volume. / Click "Payment"

On the Payment screen, enter the cash amount and click the Validate Payment button.

View order receipt

Click "Orders" Menu → Click "Orders" and list view.

View order list view

In the Order Form view, under the Products tab, display the Weight and Volume for each product line, along with the total Weight and total Volume.
