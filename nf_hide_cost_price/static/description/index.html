Hide Cost & Sale Price

Hide Cost & Sale Price helps businesses protect sensitive pricing information by restricting access to Product Cost and Sales Price across Odoo. Administrators can control which users or groups are allowed to view product pricing while hiding these values from unauthorized users.
The module hides Cost Price and Sales Price from multiple screens including Products, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, and related documents. This helps protect company profit margins, prevents unauthorized access to confidential pricing information, and improves internal security.
Whether you operate a wholesale business, manufacturing company, trading business, or retail organization, this module ensures that only authorized personnel can view sensitive pricing data.

Point Of Sale Restrict Out Of Stock

Point of Sale Restrict Out Of Stock helps businesses prevent selling products that are not available in inventory. The module automatically blocks cashiers from adding or validating products that are out of stock, ensuring that only available items can be sold through the Odoo Point of Sale.

Restrict Stock Transfer

With this module, you can easily restrict internal transfers when the available stock is less than zero or not sufficient in the source location. If a user attempts to create a transfer without enough stock, the system will raise a validation message showing the product name, source location quantity, and destination location details.

Point Of Sale Restrict Sale

In many retail environments, POS users may accidentally sell products that are out of stock or below the minimum quantity, which leads to inventory mismatches. This module solves that problem by automatically restricting sales when stock is low.
