Point Of Sale Search Product By Vendor

Point Of Sale Search Product By Vendor enhances the Odoo Point of Sale experience by allowing POS users to quickly search and filter products based on their vendor or supplier.
For businesses that manage products sourced from multiple suppliers, finding the correct product can become time-consuming, especially when the POS contains a large product catalog. This module adds a convenient vendor filter to the POS product screen, helping cashiers and sales staff narrow down the available products based on the selected supplier.
With this feature, POS users can easily locate products supplied by a particular vendor without manually searching through the entire product list. It is especially useful for businesses dealing with multi-vendor products, multiple suppliers, and large POS product catalogs.
