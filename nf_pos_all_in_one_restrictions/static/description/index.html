🚫 Restrict Stock Transfer workflow.

Go to Product
Open the product record where you can view the 'on-hand' quantity.

The On-Hand Stock view displays inventory on a per-location basis. If a product is stored in multiple locations, each location will show its respective on-hand quantity. Therefore, the stock quantity may differ between locations, reflecting the actual inventory available at each location.

Open Internal Transfer

During an internal transfer, after selecting the Source Location and Destination Location and adding the product, the system validates the available stock in the source location. When you enter the transfer quantity, if the requested quantity exceeds the available stock in the source location or the remaining stock falls below the configured minimum level, the system displays a low stock warning. This helps prevent transferring more inventory than is available from the selected source location.

🛒🚫 Point Of Sale Restrict Sale Workflow

Go to Point Of Sale → Menu "Configuration" → Click "Settings" Enable Display Stock(qty)

Stock Display: Shows the current available inventory quantity for the selected product in real time. This helps the cashier verify stock availability before completing a sale and prevents selling items that are out of stock.

The Product Form displays the On Hand Stock, which represents the total quantity of the product available across all warehouses. Users can click the On Hand quantity to view a detailed breakdown of stock by warehouse, including the available quantity in each warehouse.

Warehouse-wise Stock Visibility : The On Hand quantity displayed on the Product Form represents the total available stock across all warehouses. Clicking the On Hand quantity opens the Update Quantity view, where users can see the stock distribution by warehouse. This view displays each warehouse location along with its corresponding On Hand Quantity, providing clear visibility into inventory levels at every warehouse. The total of all warehouse quantities equals the overall On Hand stock shown on the Product Form, enabling users to monitor and manage inventory efficiently across multiple warehouse locations.

Enable Stock Restriction

When a user selects a product in the Point of Sale (POS) and the product has no available stock, the system displays a warning message and prevents the product from being added to the order.

Stock is not available and the product cannot be added.

When a product has available stock, it can be added to the POS order without any restrictions. However, if the user increases the quantity beyond the available On Hand stock, the system displays a warning message to prevent overselling.

Enable Allow to sale more than on hand

The POS system checks the product's On Hand Quantity before adding it to the order. If no stock is available for the selected product, a warning message is displayed to inform the user that the product cannot be fulfilled from the available inventory. This helps prevent incorrect sales and maintains accurate stock control.
If Allow Sale Beyond On-Hand Stock is enabled, the user can choose Order to add the product to the cart despite the stock shortage. Otherwise, the product cannot be added.
