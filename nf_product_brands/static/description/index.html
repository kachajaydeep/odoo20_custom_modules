Product Brand | Product Brand Management

The Product Brand module allows businesses to efficiently manage and organize products based on their brands within Odoo. It introduces a dedicated brand management system that enables users to create brands and assign them to products and product variants, improving product categorization and brand tracking across sales and accounting processes.
This module ensures that brand information flows seamlessly through the entire sales workflow. Once a brand is assigned to a product, it can automatically appear in sale order lines, invoice lines, and PDF reports, allowing businesses to maintain consistent brand visibility in all customer-facing documents.

The module provides flexible configuration options that allow administrators to control where brand information should be displayed. Businesses can choose to show brands in operational records like products and transactions, as well as in printed documents such as sale orders and invoices.
Additionally, the module includes an easy-to-use brand assignment wizard, enabling users to assign brands to multiple products or product variants in bulk.To maintain data integrity, the system also includes access control that prevents users from deleting brands that are already used in sales transactions, while administrators retain full control over brand management.
Overall, the Product Brand module enhances product organization, improves brand visibility in business documents, and simplifies brand management for companies working with multiple product brands.
